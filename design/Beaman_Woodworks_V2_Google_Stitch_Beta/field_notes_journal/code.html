Ref. 2024.06.12 Workshop Logs

The Anatomy of a Dovetail

Precision is often mistaken for perfection. In the Shokunin tradition, precision is the manifestation of respect. Each cut is a promise kept to the material.

To create a joint that lasts for three centuries, one must think in centuries. We consider the expansion of the wood, the humidity of the region it will reside in, and the hands that will touch it decades from now.

Ref. 2024.05.28 Craft Series

Water and Stone: Sharpening

The sound of steel on whetstone is the rhythm of the workshop morning. It is a meditative state where the senses are tuned to the microscopic burr at the edge.

Without a sharp tool, the wood is crushed, not cut. We begin every project by paying homage to our stones.
